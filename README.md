A one-page newspaper front page that sums up Tuesday 24 November 2026: 32 verified stories across 6 regions, drawn entirely in code.

**The page.** Three hero stories (IRGC says 3 tankers hit mines in Hormuz, Asia surges on the Nvidia rebound, Russian drones hit Zaporizhzhia), twenty-three more stories each in its own regional module, six one-liners, a 6-number markets strip under the headline, a UTC strip, a numbered world map, a DATE CHECK box, and a legend explaining the status tag on every story (CONFIRMED / CLAIM / REPORTED / ONGOING).
